## Interim operational treatment

1. For both providers, the project position is **NO public distribution of raw or raw-equivalent data**. The Charter and owner amendment govern raw data. The Phase 1 draft proposes an explicit public-HEAD boundary for near-verbatim data, including format conversions. Keep the provider-language observations separate from the project position.
2. Assess acquisition and export, private retention and transformation, access by collaborators or reviewers, and public outputs separately. Personal use, non-commercial purpose, a private repository and an available export feature do not establish institutional research rights. Assess aggregates, figures and genuinely derived artifacts by their lineage, reconstructability and applicable output terms. Reformatting, filtering or renaming source rows supplies no clearance.
3. Preserve the frozen Capstone v1 record. This note authorizes no acquisition, access to private archives or protected holdouts, reclassification, deletion, migration, CI or test changes, or source-data fixtures.
4. Keep **FO-4 UNSET**, **KO-2 UNVERIFIED**, **Phase 1 PROPOSED** and **Phase 2 NOT AUTHORIZED**. [Phase 1 §6](../DATA_AVAILABILITY_DESIGN.md#6-adoption--phase-2-gate) requires owner acceptance of the exact classification, removal and public-manifest scope, FO-4 and KO-2 evidence, consumer, test and checkout recovery plans, and separate Phase 2 execution authorization. Evidence review and any documentation merge are separate from those decisions.
