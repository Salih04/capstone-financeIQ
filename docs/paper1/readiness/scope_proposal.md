# Paper 1 publication-scope options

Prepared **2026-10-29**, Europe/Zurich. **Owner publication-scope choice:
UNSET.** This document proposes options; it adopts none, registers nothing and
authorizes no data access, analysis, FIQ-17 run or submission. Research support
only; not investment advice.

## Why a choice is needed

Charter §6 frames Paper 1 as measuring two look-aheads: publication timing
(measured by the PIT-v2 audit) and cohort selection (to be measured by FIQ-17
on U1). The 26 October owner addendum (FIQ-11 OD-7 = B) allows FIQ-17's
protected 2023-2025 U1 outcome calculations only **after both** the Paper 2
and the Study 3 confirmatory openings, with FIQ-17's own OSF record, committed
specification, ledger and access gates. FIQ-17 also needs FIQ-07, FIQ-10 and
FIQ-12. A paper that waits for the selection measurement therefore waits for
most of the research program.

## Option A — existing-evidence bounded audit (proposed for owner consideration)

**Question.** In a completed BIST equity-ranking study, what did an
availability-aware re-evaluation change, what can the bundled correction and
the existing controls support, and which selection-time defects are
established but unmeasured?

**Contribution claimed.** An auditable case: preserved original and corrected
results; a registered-before-own-outcomes but post-hoc correction with its
chronology; a machine-checked availability guard; explicit power, exposure and
selection limits. Not a new lagging principle, not a causal timing estimate,
not a selection-effect estimate.

**In scope (existing evidence only).**

- Numbers from `RESULTS.md` and `experiments/results_pit_v2/REPORT.md` only.
- Methods and chronology from the protocol, amendment, charter and addendum.
- FIQ-16 disclosure: structural survivor conditioning; realised-return
  selection UNKNOWN; the single-date literal-code overlap.
- The draft's corrections E1-E11, which add disclosure, not estimates.

**Out of scope.** Any selection-effect number, sign or bound; exact-filing
sensitivity (FIQ-18); dependence-robust re-inference; decomposition of the
bundled contrast; new controls or power calculations; any U1 data.

**Selection section.** Keep the NOT RUN placeholder as a declared limitation
and future-work statement, not as an empty results slot: "the selection effect
is not measured in this paper; its direction and size are unknown."

**Pre-conditions before submission.**

1. Owner closes or explicitly scopes G-1, G-4, G-6 (statements from the
   registered run record; no recomputation by this lane).
2. FO-1 statement, even if it says "unknown".
3. FO-4 rights decision, then a data-availability statement.
4. AI-use declaration and venue-specific anonymization (see
   [submission_readiness.md](submission_readiness.md)).
5. Independent scientific review (charter §5 rule 9: analyses that enter a
   paper need independent review). This audit is not that review.

**Risk.** Referees may judge a single-panel, bundled, post-hoc null with
unmeasured selection as too thin (objections R1, R5, R7, R8). The answer must
come from the audit and reproducibility contribution, not from claimed effects.

## Option B — wait for FIQ-17 selection replication

**Question.** Charter §6 as written: measure both look-aheads.

**Requires.** FIQ-07/10/12 delivered; FIQ-17 specification committed before
any run; both confirmatory openings (Paper 2 and Study 3) done first; FIQ-17's
own OSF registration, ledger and access gates. Results reported whatever they
show.

**Trade-off.** Answers R7 directly, but timing is bound to the slowest parts
of the program. The bounded audit text can still be kept as the paper's first
half.

## Option C — two outputs

Option A now as a bounded audit, and FIQ-17 later as a separate
selection-replication paper or follow-up, each with its own claims. The
second paper must not be advertised as a confirmation of the first, and the
first must not pre-announce the second's sign or size.

## Options compared

| Criterion | A bounded audit | B wait for FIQ-17 | C both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New analysis needed | None | FIQ-17 (gated) | FIQ-17 later only |
| Holdout/protected access | None | After both confirmatory openings | Later paper only |
| Matches charter §6 wording | Partly (timing measured, selection documented) | Fully | Across two papers |
| Main referee risk | Thin contribution (R5, R7, R8) | Delay | Salami-slicing perception if not distinct |
| Owner decision | Scope + venue | Scope | Scope + split |

Any of these is consistent with the charter only if the paper states plainly
what was and was not measured. The choice is the owner's.
