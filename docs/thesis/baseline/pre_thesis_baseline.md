- The evaluation window covers one unusual Turkish macro regime (high and volatile inflation), so a null result is not a general market-efficiency claim and does not transfer to other regimes or markets.
- Reproducibility is numerical-environment-qualified: byte identity requires the recorded interpreter and package versions.
